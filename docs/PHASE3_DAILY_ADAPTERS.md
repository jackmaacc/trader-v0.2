# Phase 3 pure daily adapters

`research.phase3_daily` implements deterministic **research calculations**, not a runner, account owner, simulator or broker adapter. It covers the frozen E_DONCHIAN20_V1 and C_SMA200_CONFIRM_V1 signal and sizing rules. No performance has been calculated; no execution permission is returned. It does not modify the design JSON or original design receipt. Its source hash belongs in a later implementation registration.

## Input and output API

All prices, quantities, rates, equity, gross exposure and cash use finite `Decimal`, never floats. All timestamps are timezone-aware. Dataclasses can be converted with `dataclasses.asdict`; the caller's evidence serializer must explicitly encode Decimal as exact text, and date/time as ISO values.

- `Session(day, open_at, close_at)` describes the caller's archived authoritative exchange calendar, or complete midnight-to-midnight UTC crypto day. Equity holiday completeness is a data-provenance prerequisite, not inferred from weekdays. Sessions must be ordered, unique and nonoverlapping. Crypto additionally requires every consecutive UTC date.
- `DailyBar(symbol, day, raw_close, total_return_close, received_at)` separates raw execution/sizing scale from forward total-return signal scale. Equity requires both; crypto uses venue raw close and ignores total-return values. Bars received before their completion are invalid. Bars received after a decision or dated outside its lookback cannot change its result. Multiple available versions of one bar fail as ambiguous; the caller must establish its causal revision selection.
- `decide_signal(strategy_id, symbol, bars, calendar, signal_day, decision_at, held_quantity=Decimal('0'), pending_exit=False)` returns `SignalDecision`. The equity lookback contains the signal day plus20 preceding actual sessions; entry compares strictly above preceding20, exit strictly below preceding10. Crypto uses200 days including current, with equality flat. Missing bars skip the decision. Owned assets never pyramid. Pending exits survive missing inputs or late decisions.
- Equity decisions can occur after signal close through next09:20 Eastern. Crypto decisions occur exactly00:05UTC next day. `execution_at` is next regular open or00:05UTC respectively, and `decided_at` prevents execution estimates dated before their decision for either asset class. A pending intent with a missed open needs a later actual-calendar decision; estimates do not reschedule themselves.
- `PortfolioSnapshot(equity, cash, gross, prior_utc_close_equity, entry_halted)` uses currently reconciled money and prior UTC-close equity. `entry_halted` is a required explicit boolean latched by upstream accounting after the UTC-day threshold is reached; it stays true for the rest of that UTC day even if equity recovers. This pure module cannot infer the path between snapshots or reset the latch. Missing daily baseline blocks entries, never removes exit intent. Entry halts are1% equity /3% crypto. No intraday compliance claim is possible from these snapshots. The caller must supply actual cash, excluding receivables and already-reserved amounts, and gross exposure including other reservations.
- `FeeSchedule(rate, fixed_cash=0, currency='quote')` uses a fractional rate. Verified quote-currency fees are supported for equity. Crypto uses at least25bps and explicitly supports base-asset fees: a buy deducts fees from acquired units; a sell reserves extra owned units to pay its base fee. Unknown currencies fail. Venue fee rounding/minimums beyond the supplied specification still require lifecycle verification.
- `plan_entry(decision, snapshot, fees, quantity_step=...)` freezes maximum quantity and cash budget using raw signal price. Equity quantity is whole shares and its8% target/24% aggregate cap are fixed. Crypto uses12.5%/25% and an explicitly supplied verified quantity step. Gross uses order notional conservatively, even where net acquisition is reduced by base fees. Unspent capacity is not redistributed.
- `PriceObservation(event_at, received_at, raw_open=None, bid=None, ask=None)` carries a real raw next-open observation for equity or actual quote for crypto. `estimate_entry(plan, observation, snapshot, now, stress=False, minimum_quantity=0, minimum_notional=0)` only reduces original planned quantity for cash, gross cap, fees, precision and minimums. Base/stress impact is7/14bps for equity or5/15bps for crypto, adverse to execution. Fees and impact count against frozen cash budget. Gross order quantity never increases even when price falls. Crypto requires positive noncrossed bid/ask, event age at most2seconds and execution within60seconds after00:05UTC; a still-fresh quote immediately preceding00:05 is eligible.
- `estimate_exit(decision, observation, fees, now, quantity_step=..., stress=False)` prices an owned-quantity sell. It cannot debit more owned units than available, including base-asset fees. Missing observations retain `pending_exit=True`. Even a valid hypothetical exit keeps pending true: only an independently reconciled fill ledger can reduce actual ownership. Small precision dust remains owned. No sale below venue minimum or unsupported lifecycle should be routed from these estimates.

`EntryEstimate` returns gross quantity, net acquired quantity, estimated price, cash debit and fees by currency. `ExitEstimate` returns quantity, estimated price, cash credit, total owned-unit debit, fees and pending intent. Both always state `execution_authorized=False`; these are not fill claims.

## Deliberately unimplemented orchestration

The caller still must supply causal input selection and verified hashes; all-portfolio sell-before-buy/alphabetical ordering; pending-order/exit persistence and retries; fee rounding, minimums and corporate actions; receipt provenance; cash/position/receivable ledger integration; benchmark ledgers; shared-engine full replay; stationary bootstrap; drawdown and qualification assessment; original-plan delayed scenarios; and independent reconstruction. There are no HTTP clients, file writes, clocks, orders or subscriptions in this module.

A delayed scenario must keep the original target/quantity and move execution only through an explicit actual-calendar orchestration layer. Do not recompute the signal with later information. Do not insert this research adapter into the live paper service or treat these unit tests as evidence of profitability.
