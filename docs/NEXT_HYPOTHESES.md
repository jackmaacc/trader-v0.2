## Proposed hypotheses before any backtest

The universe is fixed: SPY, QQQ, IWM, TLT and GLD. Each hypothesis is a single rule set, not a parameter grid. C means the completed daily total-return close; all lookbacks count exchange sessions. Prior-window comparisons exclude the current observation unless explicitly stated. Shared execution, sizing, risk, data and evaluation rules below apply to all four.

|---|---|---|---|---|

Sell a held ETF when it fails the same condition at a later month-end. | Month-end decision; next-session execution; holdings usually weeks or months, without a forced maximum. | **NOT YET MEASURED.** Economic rationale is not a numerical forecast. |
| H2: weekly relative-strength rotation | Persistent medium-term leadership can compensate for the cost and concentration of a small rotation portfolio. | At the last exchange close of each ISO week, rank all ETFs by C[t-21]/C[t-126]-1, descending, with alphabetical symbol tie-break. H1/H4 can hold longer than several weeks; this follows from their monthly rules and must be accepted explicitly rather than described as a guaranteed short holding period. H2 leaves at least 80% cash at target; the other candidates leave at least 75%. Actual allocation can be smaller because of whole shares, risk sizing, halts and missing data. These are research proposals using the existing conservative overnight limits, not new permission to increase exposure.

## Common deterministic research rules

- Starting capital: $100,000 cash per candidate and per benchmark, initialized once at each separately evaluated window. No external cash flows, leverage, short sales, options or borrowing. Fractional shares are not used for executable candidate quantities.
- Decision availability: use a session close only after the full session has ended and the required observations are actually available. Weekly/monthly decisions use the real exchange calendar, including holiday-shortened weeks and early closes. If a free feed is delayed, next-open execution is permitted only when the complete signal inputs arrived before that open. Otherwise skip that entry/rebalance; do not backfill a fictitious timely decision.
- Execution: submit nothing now. A later approved offline evaluation would model fills at the next actual exchange-session raw open, with adverse side-aware costs. No same-close fills or intraday high/low limit-fill assumptions. The next open is an execution price, not a signal input. Whole-share quantities are planned from the decision close, then reduced at the executable open if needed to maintain cash/risk limits; never increase quantities using knowledge of that open. Process sells before buys; order remaining buys alphabetically.
- Caps remain at their current settings: overnight/gross research exposure at most 25%, one ETF at most 10%, SPY+QQQ+IWM at most 20%, per-position modeled stop loss at most 0.1% of equity, and aggregate modeled stop risk at most 0.5%. No proposal changes the existing live or paper runner's limits. These caps are applied after the family target weights, leaving unused capital in cash rather than reallocating it.
- Protective research exit: at entry, set a fixed raw-price stop to the modeled entry fill minus three times the trailing 20-session raw-scale ATR calculated from the signal history. Resize so estimated loss to that stop, including modeled entry/exit costs, fits the risk caps. A completed close at or below the stop requests an exit at the next available open; an already-breached stop at the executable entry open cancels that entry. No assumption of guaranteed execution at the stop. Corporate splits adjust stop and quantity. Add-on units retain the original episode stop; new exposure must meet the same risk check.
- A protective exit takes priority over family signals. No same-open re-entry. All families impose the same 10-full-session cooldown after a protective exit, including the monthly allocator. H3's cooldown also applies to ordinary exits. H1/H2/H4 ordinary exits wait for the next scheduled family decision before possible re-entry. A reduction that leaves shares held is not a new episode.
- Daily risk checks occur at the completed close in this daily-data research model. A 0.5% loss relative to prior session equity or a 3% peak drawdown blocks new exposure and requests next-open liquidation. A 3% drawdown terminates that candidate's window; remain in cash through the planned end with no restart. A daily-loss block ends after the next full session; existing exposure exits first. Report actual gap losses and any observed threshold overshoot as breaches. Daily data cannot certify continuous intraday compliance with the operational mandate; a research pass cannot grant trading approval.
- Drift above an exposure cap is reduced at the next open; never treat a gap or price drift as evidence of perfect continuous cap enforcement. H4's 2-point no-trade band cannot override a cap or risk reduction. Sizing consumes the remaining capacity sequentially, without selecting the order from future outcomes.
- A held position's dividend receivable counts toward equity but cannot fund a purchase before a documented payment date. Splits change units and prices consistently. Cash earns 0% in both candidates and benchmarks; this is an explicit conservative assumption, not a claim about an available cash product. Dividends remain cash after payment until an ordinary scheduled allocation decision; no automatic reinvestment outside that rule.
- At each evaluation end, report marked portfolio equity including open holdings and receivables. Also report a separately labeled hypothetical liquidation value with exit costs for all portfolios. Do not force actual terminal exits into trade expectancy or restart the ledger daily. A trade-episode statistic alone is insufficient for a slow strategy with open positions.

## Benchmarks, expectancy and costs

**Primary comparison:** invest the same $100,000 into a static equal-weight buy-and-hold basket of SPY/QQQ/IWM/TLT/GLD, 20% each, at the first eligible raw open on exactly the candidate's start date. Hold through exactly the same end date, adjust splits, accrue cash distributions, hold paid distributions as cash, and never rebalance. Deduct costs from starting cash and scale initial quantities down proportionally to avoid borrowing. Use whole shares with residual cash for an executable comparison. No hindsight delay of the benchmark until the candidate happens to enter. Initial benchmark deployment is a comparison calculation, not an authorization to violate strategy exposure caps.

**Secondary comparison:** a static basket initially investing 25% of the same $100,000, 5% per ETF, with 75% cash and otherwise identical rules. Also report idle cash. The 25% basket matches the maximum overnight sleeve, not each candidate's realized exposure; H2 starts below that maximum. The previously implemented 50%-invested benchmark is not silently substituted for either proposed comparator.

Report gross and net total return, terminal marked/liquidation equity, annualized return, daily volatility, peak drawdown, average/max exposure, time in cash, turnover, closed episodes, win/loss amounts and observed durations. Show both benchmarks beside each candidate, including raw return difference and drawdown difference. The primary 100% basket has substantially more risk exposure. Underperforming its raw return alone does not prove absence of a risk-adjusted benefit; it does fail a claim of higher raw returns. Do not shrink or change the primary benchmark after seeing outcomes.

Define before-cost portfolio expectancy as the average daily gross P&L divided by prior-day gross equity, including cash days, plus gross terminal dollar P&L for the complete window. Separately report mean gross P&L per closed episode and its denominator, without dropping open-position P&L from the portfolio measure. Benchmark excess expectancy uses paired same-date daily portfolio returns. **Every one of these numerical quantities is NOT YET MEASURED for H1–H4.** Do not extrapolate the rejected candidates' numbers into these new hypotheses.

Proposed diagnostic cost assumptions, fixed before evaluation: 7 bps per side base and 14 bps per side stress, inclusive of assumed execution drag. They are not calibrated observations and a free data plan does not make them zero. Use the same cost convention for benchmark purchases and hypothetical terminal liquidations. An adverse-timing diagnostic executes every planned transaction one additional exchange session later without using that later price to choose trades; all positions and risk requests remain visible while delayed. These are sensitivity checks for the same frozen rules, not extra candidates or a search for the best cost setting. Unknown nonexecution operating expenses remain unknown; report business P&L as unqualified until explicitly supplied. Do not buy data or infer all operating overhead is zero from the word free.

An archived raw response may help, but its completeness has not been established here. No backtest may pretend adjusted opens are executable raw prices.

Rules for gaps: no future-based deletion of bad days, forward-filled signal prices, invented opens or inferred cash payments. A missing observation in an ETF's required lookback blocks that ETF's new signals until the complete contiguous lookback recovers. H2 requires all five valid ranking histories; H4 requires all five valid volatility histories, so an incomplete cross-section skips that scheduled rebalance for the entire portfolio. Existing holdings remain economically owned. A missing exit open leaves an exit pending to the first genuinely available open, with the delay disclosed. A missing required held/benchmark mark renders that date and window nonqualifying; stale displayed marks must be explicitly labeled. One cannot delete the date or ETF to regain qualification. Zero/invalid volatility, nonpositive prices, missing scale or invalid corporate actions reject the affected calculation.

Before any evaluation, user approval is required for the rules and these proposed dates. All four candidates remain in the attempted-family count; prior MR/MOM and other project searches remain in the research history rather than being erased by renaming.

Proposed historical eligibility: valid complete accounting/data; positive gross and base-cost net portfolio P&L; positive base-cost terminal excess over the **primary 100% basket**; positive net P&L under the fixed double-cost and delayed-execution diagnostics; no 3% drawdown termination; annualized traded notional (buys plus sells divided by time-weighted average portfolio equity, scaled to 252 sessions) no greater than 2.0. All conditions are conjunctive. This is deliberately demanding given the smaller strategy exposure. Report any drawdown benefit even when the raw-return hurdle fails; do not redefine that failure after seeing the answer.

If more than one fixed candidate meets the historical criteria, select at most one using highest base-cost terminal excess over the primary basket in the 2016–2023 development window, tie-break by lower turnover then ascending hypothesis ID. The consumed 2024–2026 window checks these same criteria and can veto selection, but cannot replace the selected candidate with another after a veto. If none qualifies, select none. Historical selection statistics are biased by selection and are not confirmation p-values.

The one selected candidate's full prospective year must satisfy the same accounting, positive-net, turnover and drawdown criteria, with positive primary-benchmark excess. At completion, assess a one-sided 95% lower confidence bound on mean paired daily net excess return using a stationary block bootstrap with block lengths 20 and 60 sessions, 100,000 resamples each and fixed seed 20260923; both bounds must exceed zero. No early success declaration, multiple candidate holdout tournament, date extension after failure, or automatic sample top-up. Fewer than 200 valid paired sessions or fewer than 12 nonoverlapping calendar-month return observations is inconclusive. A year can still be underpowered for slow strategies; resamples do not create new market history. Risk-adjusted summaries and the 25% secondary benchmark are descriptive, not substitute pass criteria.
