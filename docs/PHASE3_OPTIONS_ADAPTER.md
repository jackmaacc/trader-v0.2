# Phase 3 options rule adapter

`research/phase3_options.py` implements pure decisions for the unchanged `O_ETF_TREND_CALL_V1` design in [phase3_protocols.json](../config/research/phase3_protocols.json). It does not run a backtest, alter that design, create fills, write ownership, submit orders, or establish an edge. Returned prices are modeled arithmetic, not observed executions. Every result explicitly denies execution authority and investment qualification.

## Input contract and public API

- `Session(day, close_at)` records an actual exchange session and its timezone-aware close, including holidays and shortened sessions. `ArchivedCalendar(tuple_of_sessions, archived_sha256)` verifies ordered unique dates and the hash of exact dates/UTC close timestamps. `calendar_digest(sessions)` exposes the canonical hash calculation. Supply the archived production exchange calendar, including at least 200 warmup sessions and the next session; synthetic weekday calendars are test fixtures only. A matching hash does not authenticate provenance.
- `DailyClose` includes symbol, session date, forward total-return close, actual receipt time and an explicit `point_in_time` assertion. Every required session must have one positive close received after its actual close and by the next decision. The caller must construct total returns causally from verified corporate actions; this module cannot resolve missing payment dates or authenticate revisions.
- `Quote` includes symbol, `sip` or `opra` feed, bid/ask and sizes, market-event timestamp and receipt timestamp. Times must be aware; event time must not exceed receipt, and receipt must not exceed the decision. Price age is at most two seconds, prices positive/uncrossed, both sizes at least one, and spread no more than 10% of midpoint. Feed labels are source assertions, not entitlement verification.
- `Contract` requires explicit listing and metadata receipt timestamps, expiry, strike, call type, multiplier 100, standard/unadjusted and physical-delivery flags, and active status. These must come from point-in-time listings/deliverables; a current chain is not historical evidence. `chain_complete=True` asserts a complete point-in-time chain, not merely the currently streamed contract subset.
- `Position` identifies the actually owned single contract, original entry session and original entry premium, with an optional durable pending-exit reason. Entry premium is the total original premium for that one 100-share contract, including modeled adverse impact but excluding separately booked fees. Upstream accounting retains this amount and actual ownership; current mark is not a substitute for entry premium.

`plan_entries(calendar, session_date, *, bars_by_underlying, sip_quotes, contracts, opra_quotes, positions, equity, cash, fees_per_contract, utc_day_loss_fraction, chain_complete, fee_schedule_verified, owned_underlyings_at_signal, utc_day_entry_halted)` returns one decision per underlying in **IWM, QQQ, SPY** order. Explicit `owned_underlyings_at_signal` records ownership at the weekly signal close, so selling a position at the next open cannot invent a same-session re-entry signal. Inputs equity/cash/loss and original premiums must come from the shared, reconciled accounting engine. Verified fees are explicit currency per contract. Unknown fees/chains block entry; a 1% or larger UTC-day loss or explicit latched `utc_day_entry_halted=True` blocks entries. The caller must persist that latch for the remainder of the same UTC day even after equity recovers, and supply the UTC day identity, legitimate reset provenance and verified prior-UTC-close baseline through shared accounting. This pure adapter does not fabricate a baseline, reset a latch or infer a new day from a recovered snapshot. No account, fee schedule or quote is fetched by this module.

`weekly_trend(calendar, execution_day, symbol, bars)` is eligible only on the first actual session after the last exchange session of the previous ISO week. Friday holidays and Monday holidays follow the supplied calendar. The signal compares that previous completed close to the arithmetic mean of the 200 required closes including it. Signals become eligible at 09:35 America/New_York on the next session; UTC conversion follows DST. Missing/late/noncausal required bars and duplicate versions already available by the decision block it. Bars outside the required warmup and corrections received after the decision are excluded before duplicate validation, preserving the historical information set. No midweek entry is invented.

Qualifying contracts are filtered using the supplied point-in-time metadata and executable quote rules. Selection sorts expiry by distance from 52 calendar days (45–60 inclusive), earlier expiry on ties, then strike distance from current SIP midpoint, lower strike on ties. Symbol provides deterministic ordering only for otherwise identical metadata. The chosen contract is **not replaced with a cheaper strike** when unaffordable. Quantity is one; base modeled premium plus fees must fit 0.25% of equity and available cash. Outstanding original entry premium plus new premium must fit 0.75%; maximum three underlyings, one position per underlying. Returned plans reserve cash/premium across the ordered decisions locally without mutating caller state.

## Exit evidence and missing observations

`plan_exit(calendar, session_date, position, *, bars, option_quotes, underlying_quotes, fees_per_contract, fee_schedule_verified)` returns `hold`, `exit_candidate` or `pending_exit`. It triggers on ten completed holding sessions (entry session counts once completed), a failed weekly trend, expiry at most 21 calendar days away, or a previously retained pending exit. Missing weekly signal evidence is a coverage failure, not a fabricated sell signal. A retained pending reason survives a later trend recovery.

Starting at 09:35 through the calendar's actual close, it seeks the first observation time with qualifying, causal OPRA and SIP quotes. Each observation uses only received quotes available then; later valid quotes cannot justify an earlier execution. If no valid quote exists that session, ownership and pending intent remain. An already expired contract requires lifecycle reconciliation and never receives an invented liquidation.

Even an `exit_candidate` keeps `ownership_retained=True`: only actual or explicitly modeled fills booked by the shared engine can close an episode. Persist pending reasons and retry on following sessions upstream. The adapter cannot reconcile exercise, assignment, partial fills, missing days or service downtime.

## Cost arithmetic and integration limits

`modeled_prices(quote)` supplies per-share base buys at `ask × 1.01`, base sells at `bid × 0.99`; stress first doubles the observed half-spread around midpoint, then applies 2% adverse impact to that stressed side. Nonpositive stressed prices are rejected. Contract fees are separate. This is a deterministic interpretation of the frozen cost prose; no new cost parameters or outcomes were introduced.

Delayed cases must retain the originally selected contract and original budget and replay its later quotes through the shared execution/accounting orchestration, without reselection. This adapter does not implement the delayed campaign, delta-matched benchmark, daily marks, business-overhead accounting, fee/exercise lifecycle, paired inference or research qualification. It must not be wired directly to broker methods. The portfolio orchestrator must evaluate exits before new entries, maintain actual ownership/reservations, avoid replaying a session's plans twice, and retain failed evidence rather than dropping dates.

The exact calendar archive, full historical/prospective chain evidence, corporate-action resolution, source provenance, verified fees, executable orchestration/parity tests and independent prospective registration remain prerequisites. Unit tests use offline synthetic fixtures and do not count as prospective observations or paper trading.
